RETURN & EXCHANGE POLICY

CRITICAL REQUIREMENT: To be eligible for any return, exchange, or damage claim, you must provide a continuous, unedited unboxing video from the moment the sealed package is opened. Claims without a valid unboxing video will be strictly rejected.

7-Day Return Window

We want you to love your Mridini garments. If you are not completely satisfied, we accept returns and exchanges within 7 days of receiving your order. The items must be unworn, unwashed, unaltered, and have all original tags and packaging intact.

How to Initiate a Return

1. Ensure you have your unboxing video ready.
2. Navigate to your Account Orders or contact us directly at support@mridini.com.
3. Provide your Order ID, the reason for return, and attach the unboxing video link.
4. Once approved, our team will provide instructions for shipping the item back.

Non-Returnable Items

Custom-tailored outfits, sale items, and accessories (for hygiene reasons) are final sale and cannot be returned or exchanged unless explicitly defective upon arrival (verified via unboxing video).

Refunds

Once we receive and inspect your returned item, we will process your refund within 5-7 business days to your original method of payment. Shipping costs are non-refundable.
