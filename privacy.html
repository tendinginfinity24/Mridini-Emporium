PRIVACY POLICY

Mridini Emporium respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This policy outlines how we handle your information.

Information We Collect

When you make a purchase or create an account, we collect necessary information such as your name, billing address, shipping address, email address, and phone number to fulfill your order.

How We Use Your Information

We use your data solely to process transactions, communicate with you regarding your orders, and (if you opt-in) send you promotional offers. We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal identification information to others.

Data Security

We implement standard security measures to maintain the safety of your personal information. Payment processing is handled by secure third-party gateways; we do not store your credit card details on our servers.
